## 🔑 KEY TYPES ADDED

### For Each Major Product:

**Standard Key** (Traditional activation)
- Regular product key
- Works offline after activation
- Most common method

**Online Key** (Digital activation)
- Internet-based activation
- Cloud-linked to Microsoft account
- Usually $2 cheaper
- Quick setup

**Bind Key** (Office only)
- Bound to Microsoft account
- Easy reinstall and transfer
- Cloud backup
- Premium pricing (+$3)

**Phone Key** (Telephone activation)
- Activation via phone call
- No internet required
- Perfect for air-gapped systems
- Usually $2 more expensive
