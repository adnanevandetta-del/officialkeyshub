## 8. 💡 Pro Tips

### SEO:
- **Focus on long-tail keywords first** - "cheap windows 11 pro key" is easier to rank than "windows key"
- **Write comprehensive guides** - 2,000+ word articles rank better
- **Internal linking** - Link new articles to products and other articles
- **Update old content** - Refresh existing articles with new info

### Conversion:
- **Simplify checkout** - Fewer clicks = more sales
- **Add urgency** - "23 people viewing" creates FOMO
- **Social proof** - Display customer count and ratings prominently
- **Trust badges** - SSL, money-back guarantee, secure payment icons

### IPTV Market:
- **Swedish market is underserved** - Most IPTV content is in English
- **Sports is the killer feature** - Premier League, Champions League drives subscriptions
- **Price comparison works** - People love saving 70% vs cable TV
- **Target seasonal events** - VM 2026, SHL playoffs, etc.
