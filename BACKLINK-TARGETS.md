# Official Keys Hub — Backlink Targets (September 2026)

Replaces the old IPTV-era `BACKLINK-STRATEGY.md`, which refers to a different site.
Links are earned or submitted from your own accounts — nobody can "add" them for you. Work through these gradually (a few per week).

## Rules that keep links safe (Google link spam policy)
- No paid links, link exchanges, PBNs or "500+ directory submission" bulk lists.
- Keep exact-match anchors ("cheap windows key") under ~30% of your links. Use brand name, URL and natural phrases.
- Only list your business where the details (name, address, phone) are real and match your site. Directories and Trustpilot verify these.
- Do not present the store as an official Microsoft partner unless it is one.

## Tier 1 — Trust profiles (free, do first)
| Platform | Why it fits | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Trustpilot (trustpilot.com) | Reviews + domain-verified profile | Most important trust signal for a key store. Ask real customers for reviews. |
| Sitejabber (sitejabber.com) | Consumer review site | Claim business profile. |
| Crunchbase (crunchbase.com) | Company profile | Free contribution. |
| Google Business Profile | Local/brand listing | Only with a real, verifiable address or service area. |
| Bing Places (bingplaces.com) | Same, for Bing | Can import from Google. |
| LinkedIn company page | Brand profile | Add site URL. |
| X, Instagram, Reddit, Facebook | Already linked in your footer | Put the site URL in each bio. Mostly nofollow, but they help brand discovery. |

## Tier 2 — Business directories (free, verify eligibility)
- Manta (manta.com), ChamberofCommerce.com, Yelp, BBB — US-focused; need a matching business identity.
- Sweden: Eniro, Hitta, Proff/Allabolag — these usually need a registered Swedish company and local phone; check requirements before submitting. Allabolag/Proff pull from the company register and generally can't be self-added.

## Tier 3 — Earned links (highest value, slower)
- **Expert/journalist platforms (free, dofollow per Hawk Academy's list):** Qwoted (qwoted.com), Featured (featured.com), SourceBottle (sourcebottle.com). Answer queries about software licensing, OEM vs retail keys, Windows 10 end of support.
- **Your guides:** the activation and comparison articles on /blog (e.g. how to activate Windows 11, Office 2021 vs 365, OEM vs retail) are the natural link magnets. Pitch them to relevant IT how-to sites, PC forums and resource pages that already link to similar guides.
- **Broken-link outreach:** find dead links on Windows/Office how-to pages and suggest your matching guide.
- **Community answers:** helpful, non-spammy answers on Reddit and Quora, linking your guide only when it directly answers the question.

## Probably NOT worth submitting to
G2, Capterra, GetApp, Product Hunt, AlternativeTo, SourceForge, SaaSHub list software *vendors/products*. A reseller of other companies' licenses usually won't qualify, so skip them unless you offer your own software.

## Tracking
Record each link: date, site, URL, live/pending, anchor text. Check Google Search Console > Links monthly.
