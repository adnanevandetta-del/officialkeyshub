Open **Settings → System → Activation** and you'll see one of two lines:

- *Windows is activated with a **digital license***
- *Windows is activated with a **product key***

Both mean your Windows is genuine. The difference is what happens the next time you reinstall or change your hardware.

## The short version

| | Product key | Digital license |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | A 25-character code (XXXXX-XXXXX-…) | A record on Microsoft's servers |
| Where it lives | Email, sticker, box, BIOS | Tied to your PC's hardware ID |
| Reinstall same PC | Usually reactivates automatically | Reactivates automatically |
| New motherboard | Re-enter the key, or phone activation | Use the Activation Troubleshooter (if linked) |
| Can you lose it? | Yes, if you lose the code | Not on the same hardware |

They aren't really rivals. **A product key is how you get a digital license.** You enter the key once, Windows activates, and Microsoft records that this hardware is licensed.

## How a digital license gets created

You end up with a digital license when you:

- Activate Windows with a **product key** you bought.
- Upgraded for free from an activated Windows 10 to Windows 11.
- Bought a PC with Windows preinstalled.
- Bought the upgrade in the Microsoft Store.

## Why it matters: reinstalling Windows

With a digital license, a clean reinstall on the **same PC** is painless:

1. Boot from a Windows USB.
2. When asked for a key, click **"I don't have a product key"**.
3. Pick the **same edition** you had before (Home or Pro).
4. Connect to the internet. Windows activates by itself within a few minutes.

Full walkthrough: [How to reinstall Windows without losing your license](/blog/reinstall-windows-without-losing-license).

## Why it matters: changing hardware

Swapping RAM, a graphics card or a drive won't affect activation. Replacing the **motherboard** will, because the hardware ID changes.

That's where linking your licence to a **Microsoft account** saves you:

1. Go to **Settings → Accounts → Your info** and sign in with a Microsoft account (not a local account).
2. Open **Settings → System → Activation**. It should now say *"…with a digital license linked to your Microsoft account"*.
3. After a motherboard swap, go back to **Activation → Troubleshoot → "I changed hardware on this device recently"**, sign in, and pick your PC.

One catch: **OEM licences** are tied to the first motherboard and can't be moved this way. Retail licences can. More in [OEM vs Retail Windows keys](/blog/oem-vs-retail-windows-keys).

## Should you still keep the product key?

Yes. Save the email or write the key down. You'll want it if:

- The troubleshooter can't reactivate after a hardware change.
- You move a **retail** licence to a new PC.
- Support asks for proof of purchase.

Lost it? [How to find your Windows product key](/blog/how-to-find-windows-product-key) shows four ways to dig it up, or try our free [key finder](/key-finder).

## Need a licence in the first place?

If Settings says **"Windows is not activated"**, you don't have a digital license yet. A genuine [Windows 11 Pro key](/products/windows-11-pro) is $16.99, delivered instantly by email. Enter it in **Settings → System → Activation → Change product key** and it becomes your digital license.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a digital license the same as a product key?

No. A product key is the code you enter. A digital license is the activation record Microsoft keeps for your PC's hardware after you activate. Entering a valid key creates a digital license.

### Do I need a product key if I have a digital license?

Not to reinstall Windows on the same PC. Skip the key during setup, choose the same edition, and Windows reactivates online automatically.

### Can I transfer a digital license to a new PC?

Retail licences can be moved to a new PC, ideally after linking them to your Microsoft account. OEM licences that came preinstalled stay with the original motherboard.

### How do I link my digital license to my Microsoft account?

Sign in to Windows with a Microsoft account under Settings > Accounts > Your info. The Activation page will then say your digital license is linked to your Microsoft account.

### Does a digital license expire?

No. A digital license stays valid on that hardware for as long as you use it.
