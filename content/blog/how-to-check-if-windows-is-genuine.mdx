You bought a "pre-activated" laptop off Marketplace. Or you paid $2 for a key and it *worked*, which is suspicious. Either way, you want to know: **is this Windows actually genuine?**

Good news: you can check in about 30 seconds, no downloads needed.

## TL;DR

1. **Settings → System → Activation.** It should say *activated with a digital license* or *with a product key*.
2. Run **`slmgr /xpr`**. Genuine = "permanently activated". A date about 6 months away = probably a KMS crack.
3. Run **`slmgr /dli`**. "RETAIL" or "OEM" channel = normal. "VOLUME_KMSCLIENT" on a home PC = big red flag.

## Check 1: the Settings page

On Windows 11, open **Settings → System → Activation**. On Windows 10, it's **Settings → Update & Security → Activation**.

You want to see one of these:

- *Windows is activated with a digital license*
- *Windows is activated with a digital license linked to your Microsoft account*
- *Windows is activated using your organization's activation service* (fine on a **work** PC, sus on a personal one)

If it says **"Windows is not activated"** or you see the "Activate Windows" watermark in the corner, you don't have a valid license at all.

## Check 2: slmgr /xpr (does it expire?)

Press **Win + R**, type `slmgr /xpr` and press Enter. A small box pops up.

- **"The machine is permanently activated."** That's what a genuine retail or OEM license looks like.
- **"Volume activation will expire 5/14/2027"** or similar. That's KMS activation. Legit inside companies, but on a home PC it almost always means a **KMS crack** that renews itself in the background.

## Check 3: slmgr /dli (what kind of license?)

Run `slmgr /dli`. Look at the **Description** line:

| What it says | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| RETAIL channel | A retail key, the normal kind you buy |
| OEM_DM channel | The key built into your PC's firmware by the manufacturer |
| VOLUME_MAK channel | A business multiple-activation key |
| VOLUME_KMSCLIENT channel | Activated by a KMS server. On a home PC, likely a crack |

Real talk: if a seller's "key" ended up as VOLUME_KMSCLIENT, you didn't buy a license. You bought a script.

## Other signs it's cracked

- You were told to **turn off your antivirus** or run a `.cmd` or `.ps1` file to activate.
- There's a scheduled task or service with a random name that runs at every boot.
- A tool called **KMSpico, KMSAuto or MAS** was involved. We explain why that's a bad idea in our [MAS guide](/blog/microsoft-activation-scripts-mas-guide).
- Activation randomly disappears after an update.

## Why it matters

Cracked activators are the #1 way people get malware bundled with "free Windows". They also break after updates and can leave you without security patches if they mess with Windows Update. A genuine license avoids all of that and costs less than a takeaway.

## How to fix it

1. Get a genuine key. Windows 11 Home and Pro keys here start from the price of a pizza. Wherever you buy, check our guide to [whether cheap Windows keys are safe](/blog/cheap-windows-keys-safe) first.
2. Go to **Settings → System → Activation → Change product key** and paste it in.
3. If a KMS crack was used, remove it first: run `slmgr /upk` then `slmgr /cpky` in an admin Command Prompt, and delete any activator tasks.
4. Run `slmgr /xpr` again. You want "permanently activated".

Our [Windows 11 activation guide](/blog/how-to-activate-windows-11) walks through every error code if something goes wrong.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I know if my Windows key is genuine?

Activate it, then open Settings, System, Activation. A genuine key shows "activated with a digital license" or "with a product key", and `slmgr /xpr` says the machine is permanently activated.

### Is KMS activation illegal?

KMS is a real Microsoft system that companies use for their own PCs. Using a pirate KMS server or activator on your home PC to avoid buying a license is not legitimate and breaks Microsoft's license terms.

### Can Microsoft detect cracked Windows?

Microsoft can deactivate keys and block pirate activations through updates. Cracked PCs often lose activation after a major update.

### What does "permanently activated" mean?

It means the license never needs to renew. That's normal for genuine retail, OEM and digital licenses.

### Will a genuine key remove the "Activate Windows" watermark?

Yes. As soon as Windows activates with a genuine key, the watermark disappears, usually right away or after a restart.
