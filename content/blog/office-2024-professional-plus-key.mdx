## So what even is Office 2024 Professional Plus?

It's the most complete version of Office you can buy outright for a Windows PC. It dropped in October 2024 and replaced Office 2021, which stopped getting security updates in October 2026. Office 2021 is officially in its retirement era.

Quick lore drop: "Professional Plus" is the edition Microsoft makes for businesses. You won't find it in Microsoft's consumer store, which is why it's mostly sold through license resellers like us.

## How to not get scammed buying an Office key

A genuine key activates through Microsoft's own servers with zero extra software. Before you pay anyone, check these five things:

1. **No "activator" tools. Ever.** If the instructions say KMS, a script, a crack or "turn off your antivirus", that's not a license, it's malware with extra steps. Hard pass.
2. **You activate inside Office.** You type the 25-character key in an Office app (File → Account) or on Microsoft's setup page. That's it.
3. **The installer comes from Microsoft.** Not from some random Mega or Google Drive link.
4. **A written refund or replacement policy.** A seller who trusts their keys will swap a dud one, no drama.
5. **Message them before you buy.** If nobody replies to a simple question before you pay, nobody's replying after.

## Install and activate it (about 15 minutes)

It's genuinely easy, and you'll be done before your coffee goes cold.

1. Uninstall any older Office from **Settings → Apps**.
2. Download the official Office installer from Microsoft, following the instructions sent with your key.
3. Run it and let Word, Excel and the rest install.
4. Open Word and go to **File → Account → Change Product Key**, then enter your key.
5. You'll see "Product Activated" under Account. You're done.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Office 2024 Professional Plus a lifetime license?

Yes, it's a one-time purchase that keeps working on one PC with no expiry. Microsoft provides security updates until October 2029.

### Does Office 2024 Professional Plus work on a Mac?

No, Professional Plus is Windows-only. Mac users need Office Home 2024, Office Home & Business 2024, or Microsoft 365.

### Can I install Office 2024 on two computers?

No, one key covers one PC. To move it to a new computer, uninstall it from the old one first, then activate it on the new one.

### Does Office 2024 include Copilot?

No, Copilot AI features come with Microsoft 365 subscriptions, not with one-time Office 2024 licenses.
