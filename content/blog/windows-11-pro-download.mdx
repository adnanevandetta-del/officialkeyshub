There's no separate "Windows 11 Pro download". Microsoft ships **one installer that contains every edition**, and your **product key** decides whether you end up with Home or Pro. The download is free and comes straight from Microsoft. Here's which option to use and how to make sure you get Pro.

## TL;DR

1. Go to Microsoft's **Download Windows 11** page.
2. Use the **Media Creation Tool** for a USB stick, or download the **ISO**.
3. Enter your **Windows 11 Pro key** during setup, or choose Pro when asked. Setup installs Pro and activates it.

## The three official download options

Microsoft's Download Windows 11 page has three tools:

| Option | Use it when |
|---|---|
| **Installation Assistant** | Upgrading the PC you're on from Windows 10. Keeps your files. |
| **Media Creation Tool** | Making a bootable USB stick for a clean install or another PC. |
| **Disk image (ISO)** | Virtual machines, or making a USB yourself with a tool like Rufus. |

All three are free. Avoid ISOs from other sites: modified "pre-activated" images often include activators or malware, and they aren't licences.

## Check your PC first

Windows 11 needs **TPM 2.0**, **Secure Boot**, a **supported 64-bit CPU**, at least **4 GB RAM** and **64 GB** of storage. Microsoft's free **PC Health Check** app tells you in a minute whether your PC qualifies.

## Install Windows 11 Pro step by step

1. Download the **Media Creation Tool** and create a USB stick (8 GB or larger).
2. Boot the PC from the USB. You may need to press a key like F12 or Esc at startup to pick the boot device.
3. Choose your language and click **Install now**.
4. When asked for a product key, **enter your Windows 11 Pro key**. Setup then installs Pro.
5. Choose **Custom: install Windows only**, select the drive, and let it install.
6. Finish the setup screens. Windows activates automatically once it's online.

### "I don't have my key yet" or "setup installed Home"

If you click **I don't have a product key**, setup lets you pick the edition: choose **Windows 11 Pro**. Enter the key later in **Settings → System → Activation → Change product key**.

If the PC previously had **Windows 11 Home**, setup may install Home automatically, because Microsoft remembers the PC's Home licence. That's fine: [upgrade Home to Pro](/blog/upgrade-windows-11-home-to-pro) afterwards by entering your Pro key. Nothing needs reinstalling.

## Activate after installing

Open **Settings → System → Activation**. It should say **Active**. If not:

- Click **Change product key**, enter your Pro key, and click **Next**.
- Or use the command line: `slmgr /ipk YOUR-KEY` then `slmgr /ato`.

Full troubleshooting is in our guide to [activating Windows 11 with a product key](/blog/how-to-activate-windows-11).

## FAQ

### Is downloading Windows 11 Pro free?

Yes. The installer is free from Microsoft. You need a product key (or an existing digital licence) to activate it.

### Is there a separate Windows 11 Pro ISO?

No. The official ISO contains Home, Pro and other editions. The key you enter, or the edition you choose in setup, decides which one is installed.

### Can I install Windows 11 Pro without a key?

Yes. Choose "I don't have a product key" and pick Pro. It works unactivated with a watermark and some personalisation locked until you add a key.

### Will my Windows 10 Pro key work?

Yes. Windows 10 Pro keys activate Windows 11 Pro.
