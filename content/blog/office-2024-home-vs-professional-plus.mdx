Office 2024 comes in three editions you can buy once and keep. They look alike on a shopping page, but the app list, what you're allowed to use them for, and the platform support are different.

## TL;DR

- **Office Home 2024**: Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote. Personal use. Windows or Mac.
- **Office Home & Business 2024**: everything in Home **plus Outlook**, and you can use it for work. Windows or Mac.
- **Office 2024 Professional Plus**: the full suite, adding **Access**. Windows only.

All three are **one-time purchases** for one device, with security updates until **9 October 2029**. (Other versions: see [Microsoft end of support dates](/microsoft-end-of-support).)

## Office Home 2024: for school and life admin

Pick Home if your Office life is essays, spreadsheets, a presentation now and then, and you read email in Gmail or your browser.

- Cheapest option.
- Works on **Windows or Mac**.
- The licence is for **personal, non-commercial use**. Freelancers and small businesses should look at the next two.

## Office Home & Business 2024: Outlook, and work use

Home & Business is Home plus **Outlook**, and it's licensed for business use. That makes it the one to pick if:

- You're a freelancer or small business and want to stay within Microsoft's licence terms.
- You want the full **Outlook desktop app** for several mail accounts and calendars.
- You're on a **Mac**. It's the most complete one-time-purchase Office you can get for macOS.

## Office 2024 Professional Plus: the full suite on Windows

Professional Plus is the edition Microsoft sells to companies (officially *Office LTSC Professional Plus 2024*). It has everything in Home & Business **plus Access**, the database app.

Pick it if:

- You're on **Windows** and want every desktop app in one install.
- You need **Access** for an existing database at work or school.
- You like the classic Office setup with no Microsoft account required to activate.

It's also the edition most people end up with when they buy a key, because it's often **cheaper than Home & Business** on key sites while including more. More detail in our [Office 2024 Professional Plus key guide](/blog/office-2024-professional-plus-key).

## So which one should you buy?

1. **On a Mac?** → Home 2024 (personal) or Home & Business 2024 (work / Outlook).
2. **On Windows and need Access, or just want everything?** → Professional Plus.
3. **On Windows, personal use, no Outlook?** → Home 2024 is the cheapest pick.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Office Home 2024 include Outlook?

No. Office Home 2024 includes Word, Excel, PowerPoint and OneNote. Outlook comes with Home & Business 2024 and Professional Plus 2024.

### Can I use Office Home 2024 for work?

Office Home 2024 is licensed for personal, non-commercial use. For business use, choose Home & Business 2024 or Professional Plus 2024.

### Is Office 2024 Professional Plus available for Mac?

No. Professional Plus is Windows-only. Mac users can choose Office Home 2024 or Office Home & Business 2024.

### Does Office 2024 include Publisher?

No. Publisher isn't included in any Office 2024 edition. Microsoft ended support for Publisher in October 2026.

### How long is Office 2024 supported?

Microsoft provides security updates for Office 2024 until 9 October 2029. The apps keep working after that, but without new security fixes.

### Can I buy Office 2024 Professional Plus from Microsoft?

Not in Microsoft's consumer store. Microsoft sells Professional Plus to organisations through volume licensing, so individuals usually buy it from a key reseller. It installs with Microsoft's Office Deployment Tool. See [how to download and install Office 2024 Professional Plus](/blog/download-install-office-2024-professional-plus).
