Microsoft 365 is a subscription: stop paying and Word and Excel go read-only. Microsoft raised consumer prices in 2025 and business prices again in July 2026, and a lot of people are now asking the same thing: *can I just buy Office once?*

Yes. Here's how.

## TL;DR

- **Office 2024 is the pay-once version.** You buy it once, install it on one PC, and it keeps working. Security updates run until **October 9, 2029**.
- **Pick the edition by the apps you use:** Home for Word, Excel and PowerPoint; Home & Business if you need Outlook; Professional Plus if you also need Access.
- **Your files don't change.** Documents made in Microsoft 365 open in Office 2024 as normal.
- **Free option:** Office on the web works in a browser with a Microsoft account, with fewer features.

## What you give up by leaving Microsoft 365

Be honest with yourself about these before you switch:

- **1 TB of OneDrive storage.** Office 2024 still works with OneDrive, but you only get the free 5 GB unless you pay for storage separately.
- **New features.** Office 2024 gets security fixes, not new features. What you install is what you keep.
- **Several devices.** Office 2024 is licensed for **one PC**. Microsoft 365 Personal covers all your devices for one person.
- **Copilot AI** inside Word and Excel.

If none of those matter to you, Office 2024 costs far less over a few years. We did the maths in [Office 2024 vs Microsoft 365](/blog/office-2024-vs-microsoft-365).

## How to switch from Microsoft 365 to Office 2024

1. **Make sure your files are saved locally**, or in OneDrive folders you can still reach. Anything over 5 GB in OneDrive needs moving before the subscription ends.
2. **Buy the Office 2024 edition** that matches the apps you use.
3. **Uninstall Microsoft 365**: Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Microsoft 365 → Uninstall. Two Office versions on one PC conflict.
4. **Install Office 2024** and enter your key. The full walkthrough is in [How to download and install Office 2024 Professional Plus](/blog/download-install-office-2024-professional-plus).
5. **Cancel the subscription** in your Microsoft account under *Services & subscriptions*, so it doesn't renew.

## Is there a "lifetime" Office license?

Office 2024 is the closest thing. It's a perpetual license, so it never expires, but Microsoft stops sending security updates after October 9, 2029. Be wary of anything sold as a "lifetime Microsoft 365" or "lifetime Office 365" key: 365 is a subscription, so a lifetime version doesn't exist.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I still buy Microsoft Office without a subscription?

Yes. Office 2024 (Home, Home & Business and Professional Plus) is sold as a one-time purchase for one PC, with security updates until October 9, 2029.

### What happens to my files if I cancel Microsoft 365?

Your documents stay yours. Files saved on your PC are untouched, and files in OneDrive stay there, though you drop back to the free 5 GB of storage. If you're over that, move files out before the subscription ends.

### Does Office 2024 include Outlook?

Office Home & Business 2024 and Office 2024 Professional Plus include Outlook. Office Home 2024 doesn't.

### Can I use Office 2024 on more than one computer?

No. Each Office 2024 license is for one PC. If you move to a new PC you can transfer it. See [how to move your Office license to a new PC](/blog/move-office-license-to-new-pc).

### Is Office on the web really free?

Yes, with a free Microsoft account. It covers basic editing in Word, Excel and PowerPoint in your browser, but lacks many desktop features and needs an internet connection.
