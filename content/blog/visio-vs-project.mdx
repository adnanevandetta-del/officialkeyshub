Visio and Project sound similar and both sit in Microsoft's "work" family, but they solve completely different problems. **Visio draws things. Project plans things.** Once you see it that way, the choice is usually obvious.

## The short answer

- **Microsoft Visio** creates diagrams: flowcharts, process maps, org charts, network diagrams, floor plans and more.
- **Microsoft Project** plans and tracks projects: tasks, deadlines, dependencies, Gantt charts and who's working on what.

## Side-by-side

| | Visio | Project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main job | Visualise how things work or connect | Plan and track work over time |
| Typical output | Flowchart, org chart, network or floor plan | Gantt chart, timeline, resource plan |
| Who uses it | Analysts, IT, engineers, HR, facilities | Project managers, PMOs, team leads |
| 2024 version supported until | October 9, 2029 | October 9, 2029 |

## Choose Visio if you need to…

- Map a business process or approval flow.
- Draw your network, server room or cloud architecture.
- Build an org chart or a floor plan.
- Create technical diagrams such as UML or database models (Visio Professional).

## Choose Project if you need to…

- Break a project into tasks with deadlines and dependencies.
- See the critical path and what happens when something slips.
- Assign people and track how busy they are.
- Report progress to stakeholders with timelines and Gantt charts.

## Do teams use both?

Often, yes. A typical IT rollout uses **Visio** to diagram the new network and **Project** to schedule the work of building it. If that sounds like you, buy both. Each is a separate license.

## Standard or Professional?

- **Visio Professional** adds more diagram types and templates than Standard, including advanced technical diagrams, plus more collaboration features. Most business users pick Professional.
- **Project Professional** adds team and resource-management features over Standard, and can connect to Project Online and Project Server. Choose Professional if you manage people, not just your own tasks.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can Visio make Gantt charts?

Visio has a basic Gantt chart template for drawing one, but it doesn't calculate schedules or dependencies. For real project planning, use Project.

### Is Visio included in Office?

No. Visio and Project are sold separately from Office, including Office 2024 Professional Plus.

### Which is easier to learn, Visio or Project?

Visio is easier: you drag shapes onto a page. Project takes longer to learn because it calculates schedules from tasks, durations and dependencies.

### Are Visio 2024 and Project 2024 one-time purchases?

Yes. Both are one-time purchases for one PC, with security updates until October 9, 2029.
