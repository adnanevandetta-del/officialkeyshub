## How to spot a bad Black Friday key deal

These checks work on any store, including this one.

1. **Fake countdown timers.** Reload the page. If the "deal ends in 2:59:59" clock starts over, the deadline isn't real.
2. **No refund policy, or a vague one.** A real seller tells you what happens if a key doesn't activate. Ours is a [30-day money-back guarantee](/refund-policy).
3. **Payment only by gift card or bank transfer.** Pick a method that lets you dispute a charge, such as PayPal or a card.
4. **"Lifetime Office 365" for $9.** Microsoft 365 is a subscription, so there's no such thing as a lifetime key for it. Office 2024 is the one-time version.
5. **Keys for the wrong region or edition.** Check that the product name matches exactly what you need, for example Windows 11 **Pro**, not Pro Education or N.
