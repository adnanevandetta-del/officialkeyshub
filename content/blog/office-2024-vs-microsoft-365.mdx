Microsoft *really* wants you on a subscription. Every Office download page nudges you toward Microsoft 365. But "should I pay once or pay every year" is a fair question, and the honest answer depends on how you use Office.

Here's the breakdown, no upsell energy.

## TL;DR

- **Office 2024**: pay once, keep it on one PC, security updates until **October 2029**. No new features, no cloud storage.
- **Microsoft 365**: pay every year. You get the newest features, **1 TB of OneDrive**, Copilot, and the apps on PC, Mac, phone and tablet.
- If Office is just "Word for assignments and Excel for budgets", **Office 2024 wins on price**, by a lot.

## The side-by-side

| | Office 2024 | Microsoft 365 Personal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How you pay | Once | Every year |
| Devices | 1 Windows PC | PC, Mac, phone, tablet |
| Updates | Security fixes until Oct 2029 | New features every month |
| Cloud storage | None included | 1 TB OneDrive |
| Copilot AI | No | Yes |
| Works offline | Yes, fully | Yes, but it checks in online |
| Stops working if you stop paying | No | Yes, apps go read-only |

## Let's talk money

Microsoft 365 Personal costs **$99.99 a year** in the US at the time of writing. That sounds fine until you add it up:

| Time | Microsoft 365 Personal | Office 2024 Pro Plus (here) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 year | $99.99 | $34.99 |
| 3 years | ~$300 | $34.99 |
| 5 years | ~$500 | $34.99 |

Even Microsoft's own price for Office Home 2024 ($149.99) pays for itself in under two years compared with the subscription. That's why the pay-once option is quietly the better deal for most single-PC users.

## Pick Office 2024 if…

- You use **one Windows PC** for school, work or life admin.
- You don't need cloud storage (or you already use Google Drive, iCloud or Dropbox).
- You're tired of subscriptions eating your bank account one $9.99 at a time.
- You want something that **just works offline**, forever, with no "renew now" pop-ups.

Not sure which Office 2024 edition? Home has Word, Excel and PowerPoint. Home & Business adds Outlook. **Professional Plus** is the full suite with Access too. Our [Office 2024 Professional Plus guide](/blog/office-2024-professional-plus-key) goes deeper.

## Pick Microsoft 365 if…

- You switch between **PC, Mac, iPad and phone** and want the full apps everywhere.
- You actually need **1 TB of cloud storage** and use it.
- You want **Copilot** built into Word and Excel.
- Your company or school needs Teams and shared mailboxes. In that case, look at a Business plan.

## "But Office 2024 doesn't get new features"

True. But be honest: when was the last time a new Office feature changed your life? Office 2024 already has dynamic arrays, XLOOKUP, dark mode, and the modern look. It's **not** going to get Copilot. If you don't care about AI writing your emails, you're not missing much.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Office 2024 a one-time purchase?

Yes. You pay once and use it on one Windows PC for as long as you like. Microsoft provides security updates until October 9, 2029, and the apps keep working after that.

### Can I switch from Microsoft 365 to Office 2024?

Yes. Uninstall Microsoft 365 first, then install Office 2024 with your new key. Your documents stay the same because both use the same file formats.

### Does Office 2024 include OneDrive?

You can use the free 5 GB OneDrive plan with any Microsoft account, but Office 2024 does not include the 1 TB that comes with Microsoft 365.

### Is Office 2024 cheaper than Microsoft 365?

Over time, yes, by a lot. One Office 2024 key costs less than a single year of Microsoft 365 Personal, and you never pay again.

### Does Office 2024 work on Mac?

The Professional Plus edition is Windows-only. Mac users should look at Office Home 2024 for Mac or Microsoft 365.
