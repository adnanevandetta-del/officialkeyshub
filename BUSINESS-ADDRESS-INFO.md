## Legal Requirements (Sweden)

To operate legally in Sweden:

1. **Register with Bolagsverket** (Swedish Companies Registration Office)
   - https://bolagsverket.se/
   - Cost: ~1,500 SEK registration fee

2. **Register for VAT** (if turnover > 30,000 SEK)
   - Through Skatteverket (Swedish Tax Agency)
   - https://skatteverket.se/

3. **Get F-Skatt** (Tax certificate for companies)
   - Required for invoicing

4. **Business Bank Account**
   - Swedish business bank account required

5. **Insurance**
   - Business liability insurance recommended
