
## ⚠️ What to AVOID

### DON'T:
1. ❌ Buy backlinks (Google penalty)
2. ❌ Use link farms
3. ❌ Spam forums
4. ❌ Auto-generate links
5. ❌ Link to/from low-quality sites
6. ❌ Over-optimize anchor text
7. ❌ Get all links at once (looks unnatural)

### DO:
1. ✅ Build links gradually (5-10 per week)
2. ✅ Focus on quality over quantity
3. ✅ Diversify link types
4. ✅ Create valuable content
5. ✅ Build relationships
6. ✅ Track your links
7. ✅ Disavow toxic links
